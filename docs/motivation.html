Motivation

Why This Project

Every student has seen proxy attendance happen, and many QR attendance systems already used in colleges are easy to beat: the code is static, or a screenshot works for the whole lecture. That made attendance a good real-world setting for the course material, because each attack maps directly onto a cryptographic concept.

Mapping attacks to course concepts

Everyday cheating methodSecurity propertyCourse concept used
Screenshot of the QR sent on WhatsAppFreshnessTime-windowed MAC (HMAC)
Making a QR for the next slotAuthenticityKeyed MAC, secret key
Logging in as a friendEntity authenticationDigital signatures (ECDSA)
Resending a captured requestReplay protectionNonces and timestamps
Changing records laterIntegrityHash chains (SHA-256)
Denying or forging a reportNon-repudiationDigital signatures (Ed25519)
Guessing passwordsConfidentiality of credentialsPassword hashing (Argon2id)

Design principle

Use only standard, well-studied primitives from established libraries, and put the effort into the protocol: what is signed, what is checked, in which order, and what is logged. No custom cryptography.
